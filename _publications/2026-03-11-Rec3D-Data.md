The dataset linked includes the standard reconstructions with 300<sup>3</sup> voxels for 10 different porosities, the same structures that were presented in "Efficient reconstruction and validation of heterogeneous microstructures for energy applications," published in the International Journal of Energy Research, 46 (2022) 22757-22771.

For more information, please refer to the dataset publication [here](https://digitalporousmedia.org/published-datasets/drp.project.published.DRP-1129v2).
