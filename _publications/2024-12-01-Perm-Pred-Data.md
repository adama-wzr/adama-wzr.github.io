The dataset linked contains the following:
* Original microstructure data, 4500 total images
* External test set, 400 total images
* Permeability for the entire set
* Pressure and velocity maps for the entire set
* Trained CNN models for permeability prediction:
    * AlexNet
    * DenseNet
    * ResNet34
    * ResNet50
    * VGG19
    * VGG19 (top 10 Ensemble)
