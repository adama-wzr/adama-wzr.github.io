Invited talk for a department-wide seminar. The talk focused on leveraging resources from the former Extreme Science and Engineering Discovery Environment (XSEDE) program, now Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services & Support (ACCESS), to maximize efficiency and impact in computational materials science research.
